The Garden of Us

It started with a name and a recommendation from a friend—a simple bridge built toward someone I didn’t know would eventually become my world. When you accepted and we finally began to talk, the days started to feel different. Every conversation was a new discovery, a new note in a melody. I even built a playlist inspired by you, a collection of sounds that felt like your energy before we had even met in person.

I remember our first date vividly, not because it was perfect, but because it was ours. The sky was heavy that day, and the rain didn’t just fall—it poured. We both arrived at your home soaked to the bone, laughing off the weather that tried to stop us. That indoor date, sheltered from the storm, was the moment I knew.

"In the middle of the rain, I found my sunshine."

Through the seasons, I’ve learned the beautiful architecture of who you are. I know that your world is painted in baby pink, black, and silver.I know that a jungle green range rover captivates your eyes like no other. I know that when Kehlani sings "Everybody's Business," it hits differently for you. I know your heart’s deepest fears and your simplest joys—how a random M-Pesa message or a TikTok dance can brighten your spirit, and how much you treasure the family you’re so afraid to lose.

We’ve had our storms—the kind that don't come from the sky. We’ve had the arguments, the misunderstandings, and the heavy silences. But every single time, we’ve looked at each other and chose us. We let our love be the compass, silencing our egos to let our hearts lead the way.

We don’t have a map for the future, but as long as we are walking it together, I’m not afraid. What started as a single seed of a conversation has flourished into something beautiful. And I know, with time, our love will grow from this moment into a vast, eternal garden.
